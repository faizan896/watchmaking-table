The reason this exists

I loved watches long before
I could make anything.

I read the histories. Every maison, every founder, every calibre that mattered — I knew the stories before I knew the words for what I was looking at.

Then I started watching the watchmakers. Hours of it. A pair of hands under a lamp, seating a bridge, driving a screw a quarter turn and stopping. I could tell you what an escapement does. I had never made one thing.

That is the gap this studio is for. I had the eye and no hands — no CAD, no drawing, no bench. So I built the bench instead: every real decision a watchmaker makes, laid out so that anyone who loves this can finally make something of their own.

Faizan
